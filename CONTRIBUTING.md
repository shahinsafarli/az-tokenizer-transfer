## Ownership and review

CODEOWNERS places each source file's primary owner and a different eligible alternate on the same rule. Specific rules follow the broad fallback. The last matching rule wins; repeated patterns replace earlier ownership. Either listed owner can satisfy a single code-owner requirement; this is not two independent approvals. The registry also defines eligible reviewers and approval counts. A PR author cannot approve their own PR.

The normal plan requires a real non-author review of the current head and successful required checks. If explicit user instructions authorize automated approval, disclose it as automated and record the exact head. Do not claim the account holder personally reviewed it. This is an authorized process variation, not evidence of independent human review. Stale approvals, unresolved change requests and failed checks block merging. Preserve main protection.

## Validation and merge

Run registered scope and plan checks, applicable CI, and additional validations specified for the PR. Record only checks actually performed. Tie supplied evidence to its file hashes and revision. Early governance PRs do not require model downloads, training or PDF builds; later stages must satisfy their own readiness and validation requirements.

Use the existing external executor's help for commands. Opening and merging are separate operations unless both are explicitly requested. Before merging, verify exact head, dependencies, registered files, identity, current approvals, CI and protection. Squash with the planned title, verify the resulting author and absence of co-author trailers, then synchronize local main without discarding changes.

Use the bug form for defects and the finding form for evidence discrepancies. Include paths, revisions and redacted outputs. Preserve licensing restrictions and unresolved permissions in NOTICE and release checks.
